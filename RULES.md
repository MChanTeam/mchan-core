# MChan Community Rules

**Effective 2026-08-10**

These are MChan's site-wide minimum rules. They apply on every board. A board may
set stricter topic, format, or quality rules, but no board rule may weaken these
minimums.

## Prohibited content

Do not post or use MChan for:

- Illegal content.
- Doxxing and personal information.
- Credible threats.
- Targeted harassment.
- Spam.
- Malware.
- Explicit sexual media and pornography.
- Sexual solicitation.
- Abuse of the service.
- Anonymous or unverifiable accusations against identifiable private
  individuals.

Public figures, public organizations, universities, companies, and documented
public events may be discussed when the discussion is lawful and relevant. This
does not permit threats, targeted harassment, doxxing, or other prohibited
content.

## Good-faith use

Do not evade a board ban, site-wide ban, CAPTCHA challenge, or rate limit. Do not
impersonate another person or organization. Do not manipulate reports, including
by submitting coordinated or knowingly misleading reports to distort moderation.

## Posting is permanent to the author

Check a post before publishing. Posts cannot be edited after publication, and
authors cannot self-delete published posts. A post may remain publicly readable,
including in an archive, unless moderation or the thread lifecycle changes its
visibility. Moderators may hide content when required; posting anonymously does
not make content completely untraceable or guarantee immunity from
identification.

## Reporting

Report a thread or reply when it violates these rules or a board's stricter
rules. Identify the specific post and explain the relevant violation in good
faith. Do not use reports to settle personal disputes, harass someone, or
manufacture a violation.

Reports enter the protected moderator queue. A moderator may dismiss or resolve
a report without changing the reported content's status. A moderator may hide,
remove, or quarantine reported content; in the current system these actions hide
it from public reads while the content remains stored, and they are not a
guarantee of physical deletion. A moderator may lock a visible thread, which
keeps it readable but prevents new replies.

Moderators may also issue temporary bans: a board ban may last 1–30 days, and a
site-wide ban may last 1–365 days, when the available protected moderation
information supports the action. Moderation actions and bans are recorded in the
moderation system.
